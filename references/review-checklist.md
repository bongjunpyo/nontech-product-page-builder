## Final Review Checklist

Before uploading this page to your online store, please check each item below.

**Product information**
- [ ] Is the product name correct?
- [ ] If a price is shown, is it accurate?
- [ ] Do the listed product benefits match what your product actually does?
- [ ] Are there any phrases that should not be used? Remove them before uploading.

**Images**
- [ ] Are all the photos you wanted to include present?
- [ ] Have you replaced the placeholder image filenames with your actual image files?
- [ ] Are patent, certification, or award images accurate and up to date?

**Trust and accuracy**
- [ ] Are all certification, patent, and award details correct?
- [ ] Are all numbers (performance specs, dimensions, weight, capacity) verified?
- [ ] Is the country of origin correct?
- [ ] Are the materials or ingredients listed accurately?

**Policies**
- [ ] Are shipping conditions correct?
- [ ] Are exchange and return conditions correct?

**Look and feel**
- [ ] Does the page match your company's atmosphere and values?
- [ ] Does it look good on a mobile phone? (Open it on your phone to check)
- [ ] Does the buy or inquiry button text sound natural?

**Before uploading**
- [ ] Is there any remaining information you still need to verify?
- [ ] Have you shown this page to someone who knows your product well?

---

Once everything checks out, you're ready to upload this page to your store.

If you need changes, let me know what to adjust and I'll update the HTML for you.
